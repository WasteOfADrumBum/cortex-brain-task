Privacy policy

Cortex is a personal dashboard owned and used by one person, Joshua Small. This page explains what it does with Google data. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Who uses Cortex

Cortex has a single user, its owner. It has no public sign-up, no other accounts and no advertising. It does not collect information about visitors.

What Cortex does with Google data

  • Google Drive, only files Cortex creates (drive.file): Cortex saves its own files in the owner's Drive, such as monthly finance summaries under /Cortex/finance, the state of approvals and a log of approved changes. It cannot see or change any other Drive file through this access.
  • Google Drive, read-only in the browser (drive.readonly): when the owner links Drive, the browser reads the owner's notes and morning briefings to show them on the dashboard. Nothing is changed.
  • Google Calendar events (calendar.events): used only when the owner approves a proposed change, to add or move that one event. Cortex never deletes events.

Sharing and storage

  • Google data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Data stays in the owner's own Google account. The server keeps only access tokens, as private settings on its host, and does not keep copies of files or events.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Third-party apps and services page. Files Cortex created stay in Drive until deleted there.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on Cortex's Google sign-in screen.
